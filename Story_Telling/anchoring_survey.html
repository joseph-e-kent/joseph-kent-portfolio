Anchoring Survey Experiment

Overview

This project examined the anchoring effect by testing whether different starting numbers influenced participants’ estimates of the height of the tallest redwood tree.

What I Did

  • Collected survey data from two anchor conditions
  • Compared group estimates using summary statistics, visualizations, and a t-test
  • Cleaned the data by filtering extreme outliers and calculated an anchoring index

Results

Participants who received the higher anchor gave much larger estimates on average. The results supported the idea that numerical anchors can strongly influence judgment.

Tools Used

R, ggplot2, Hypothesis Testing, Data Cleaning
